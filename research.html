Research

Scientific background, objectives, methodology, and related publications for the TRExt project.

Project Summary

TRExt (TRE Text Analytics) evaluates and extends the DARE UK TREvolution infrastructure to support natural language processing and data model mapping of unstructured text within Trusted Research Environments.

Unstructured text — including clinical correspondence, discharge summaries, radiology reports, and transcribed interviews — contains rich, sensitive information not currently accessible through federated analytics pipelines. TRExt assembles state-of-the-art NLP tools and data standardisation tools into a pipeline deployable within TRE-FX, the federated execution layer of the TREvolution programme. Outputs are mapped to OMOP and CDISC standard data models, with full provenance metadata captured, enabling research-ready analysis across distributed TRE environments while preserving privacy.

Public Involvement & Engagement (PIE) activities are embedded throughout all stages of the project. See the PIE Activities page for details.

Research Themes

WP1

Clinical NLP within TRE-FX

Deploying the Mental Health Text Analytics Cloud (MH-TAC) and CogStack within TRE-FX to enable named entity recognition and relation extraction from unstructured clinical text. MH-TAC — a containerised platform-as-a-service hosting over 100 NLP models — and CogStack, an EHR Extract-Transform-Load system installed across 6 UK NHS Trusts, provide complementary, clinically validated NLP capabilities across a broad range of clinical disciplines.

Deliverables
  • D1.1: Deployable NLP solution within TRE-FX (M6)
  • D1.2: Integrated NLP pipeline for text standardisation (M12)
WP2

Data Mapping & Standardisation

Deploying Lettuce (an LLM-assisted OMOP concept mapping tool), Carrot (an OMOP data transformation toolkit), and the Unison data virtualisation platform within TRE-FX. Together these tools convert NLP-extracted concepts into OMOP CDM and CDISC SDTM standard formats — with a 'human in the loop' approval step — enabling federated analysis of text-derived structured data across TREs without requiring physical data duplication.

Deliverables
  • D2.1: Deployable OMOP mapping solution within TRE-FX (M6)
  • D2.2: OMOP-to-CDISC mapping via Unison within TRE-FX (M9)
  • D2.3: Integrated text standardisation pipeline (M12)
WP3

Project Management & PIE

A structured programme of project management (PMT, Advisory Board, weekly standups, monthly risk reviews) combined with a full Public Involvement and Engagement (PIE) programme. PIE is led by a dedicated specialist at SAIL Databank, running online and hybrid workshops with members of the public to co-design technical methodologies and capture public trust, concerns, and recommendations that directly inform project decisions.

Deliverables
  • D3.1: Kick-off hackathon (M1)
  • D3.2: RRI action plan (M3)
  • D3.3: PIE workshops (M4)
  • D3.4: PIE findings report (M6)
  • D3.5: Final technical report (M12)

Methodology

TRExt's methodology spans four interlocking areas — data, NLP, standardisation, and provenance — combining open datasets, clinically-validated tools, and FAIR practices.

🗄️

Data Gathering

Project development uses fully open or synthetic data — specifically MIMIC-III and MIMIC-IV (publicly available de-identified clinical datasets) and synthetic/AI-generated fake clinical text. Validation uses the de-identified CRIS dataset (South London and Maudsley NHS Foundation Trust EHR), which has established ethics approval and governance. In production TRE deployments, unstructured text processing is undertaken entirely by the TRE provider; researchers never access the raw unstructured data.

⚙️

NLP Pipelines

TRExt employs two complementary NLP systems. The CRIS NLP service (MH-TAC), developed for the DATAMIND programme, is a containerised Azure-based platform hosting over 100 NLP models for extracting entities and relations from mental health and general clinical text. CogStack, an EHR Extract-Transform-Load system deployed at 6 UK NHS Trusts and two international providers, is used alongside MH-TAC to extend NLP capability across clinical disciplines. Both systems use MedCAT for SNOMED-CT concept and relation extraction.

🔗

Data Standardisation

Extracted clinical concepts are mapped to the OMOP Common Data Model (CDM) using Lettuce — an LLM-assisted mapping tool that suggests OMOP vocabulary concepts for source terms — and Carrot, which executes the physical data transformation into OMOP CDM. Interoperability with CDISC SDTM is achieved through the Unison data virtualisation platform, which creates a virtual mapping between OMOP and CDISC, eliminating the need for physical data duplication while enabling CDISC-format analysis within TRE-FX.

🛡️

FAIR Data & Provenance

All datasets are documented according to FAIR principles (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable). TRExt captures provenance metadata at every stage of the NLP and mapping pipeline using the Five Safes RO-Crate metadata framework, developed in DARE UK TREvolution. This ensures that every analytical output can be traced back to its source, supporting transparency and reproducibility of federated text analytics.

Standards & Frameworks

🛡

Five Safes Framework

All TRE operations follow the Five Safes: safe people, safe projects, safe settings, safe data, and safe outputs. TRExt implements this via the TREvolution 5-Safes Task Execution Service.

📋

FAIR Data Principles

All datasets and workflows are Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable. Provenance metadata is captured throughout the TRExt pipeline using Five Safes RO-Crate.

⚖

UK GDPR & Data Protection Act 2018

All activities comply with UK General Data Protection Regulation and the Data Protection Act 2018. CRIS NLP processing has ethics approval from South London and Maudsley NHS Foundation Trust.

👥

DARE UK PIE Guidelines

All public involvement and engagement activities align with the DARE UK PIE Guidelines and the PEDRI (Public Engagement in Data Research Infrastructure) Good Practice Standards.
